## About Me

I specialize in designing, developing, monitoring, and optimizing data
integration and analytics solutions. At Tata Consultancy Services, I worked
with Informatica PowerCenter, SQL, Python, Tableau, Autosys, ServiceNow,
JIRA, and enterprise data-transfer systems to support development and
production environments.

My experience includes ETL pipeline development, SQL optimization, data
validation, workflow monitoring, incident resolution, requirements gathering,
UAT, deployment, and post-production support across the complete Software
Development Life Cycle.

Alongside enterprise data engineering, I have developed AI-powered automation
projects using n8n, Make.com, Google Gemini AI, OpenAI APIs, LangChain,
Google Sheets, Gmail, Google Drive, and Looker Studio.

I recently completed my Master of Science in Management Information Systems
from Auburn University at Montgomery and am pursuing opportunities in
**Data Engineering, ETL Development, Data Analytics, and Analytics Engineering**.
